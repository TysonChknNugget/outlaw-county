Outlaw tips

  • Hold right mouse until the crosshair turns gold for a precise shot that hits much harder.
  • Crouch (C) behind crates, barrels, rocks or wagons and bullets from the other side hit the cover. Roll (Space) through gunfire.
  • Landing hits fills your Nerves. Press V to slow time for a few seconds.
  • Wanted posters at the sheriff's office pay more if you bring the outlaw in alive: knock them out with your fists or make them surrender, then hogtie them.
  • Crimes need witnesses. Civilians run to the law — stop them and the crime goes unreported.
  • Break line of sight, hide in bushes or buildings, or ride far away to lose a search.
  • Pay off your bounty at a train station or sheriff's office when nobody is chasing you.
  • Hold right mouse and press E near a clerk to rob a store. Trains can be robbed while stopped at a station.
  • Sell pelts at general stores or the trapper, and stolen goods to Old Silas at Juniper Canyon Camp.
